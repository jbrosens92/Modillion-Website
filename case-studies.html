The Mill

A 72-unit apartment community with approximately 28,700 square feet of ground-floor commercial space and a 1.3-acre developable land parcel. The buildings were constructed between 1881 and 1981 and significantly renovated in 2021.

Business Plan

Renovate the original twelve apartments at 328 Pemberwick and convert a non-income unit into a thirteenth apartment, improve commercial lease terms and re-tenant vacancy, and prepare the 1981 building for condominium conversion.

45 Market

Commercial
~19,000 SF
Acquired
January 2026

A 2020-built, five-story mixed-use property in downtown Morristown, New Jersey, comprising 54 luxury apartments over approximately 19,000 square feet of street-level commercial space. The residences range from studios to two bedrooms and are served by a penthouse resident lounge, outdoor roof deck, and fitness center. The property sits steps from the Morristown Green and within walking distance of the Morristown train station, which offers direct service to New York Penn Station. Both the residential and commercial space are fully leased.

Business Plan

Acquire the interest of an existing limited partner at a discount to net asset value and hold the property, benefiting from stabilized occupancy across both the residential and commercial components.
