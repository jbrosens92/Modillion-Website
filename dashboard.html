Tell the agent

Describe a conversation and it becomes a log entry — investor, date, channel, deals discussed, check size, next step. Give it an instruction instead and it becomes a set of edits: drop East Blocks from their list, mark them passed, set owner to JB, that deal fell through, archive them. Either way you see a draft first and nothing is saved until you say so. Corrections you make are remembered. This box is the careful route — every field of the draft is editable before it is saved. For a back-and-forth, or to change something already logged, use the agent in the corner; it works on every tab.
